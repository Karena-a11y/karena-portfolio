THE EVAH – Brand Identity & Graphic Design

THE EVAH is a conceptual speakeasy jazz club designed as an escape from the pace of Nairobi life. The brand combines intimate luxury, authentic live jazz, and a relaxed atmosphere, blending a classic jazz aesthetic with a modern, sophisticated identity.

01 — Hero

02 — Moodboard

A visual representation of the atmosphere envisioned for THE EVAH — intimate, luxurious, earthy, and sophisticated, with a modern interpretation of classic jazz.

03 — Colour Palette

A rich, earthy colour palette chosen to reflect the warmth, sophistication, and relaxed atmosphere at the heart of THE EVAH.

04 — Typography System

A carefully selected typography system combining classic elegance with a modern touch to complement the character of the brand.

05 — Logo System

A carefully crafted wordmark inspired by the meaning of THE EVAH — “to give life.” The two laurel leaves beneath the wordmark symbolize luxury and victory, brought together through the brand's rich, sophisticated colour palette.

06 — Final Logo

07 — Instagram Feed

Translating the visual identity into a cohesive social media presence designed to communicate the atmosphere and personality of THE EVAH.

08 — Drinks Menu

Extending the brand identity into the guest experience through a drinks menu that reflects the club's sophisticated and intimate atmosphere.

09 — Artist Lineup

Applying the visual identity to event communication while keeping the presentation elegant, clear, and consistent with the jazz club's character.

10 — Event Poster

A promotional application designed to capture the energy of live jazz while maintaining the refined visual language of THE EVAH.

11 — Membership Card

A branded membership card designed to extend the identity into a tangible part of the THE EVAH experience.
